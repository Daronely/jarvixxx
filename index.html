CRAFT

Our four engineering pillars

These principles come from professional practice — no product ships without passing them.

Craft over noise

Every pixel and every line of code needs a reason. We'd rather build one thing right than ten things half-way.

CRAFT OVER NOISE

Meaningful motion

Animation is worth it only when it aids understanding — never decoration. Every move has a communicative goal.

MEANINGFUL MOTION

Performance first

Every product must run smoothly on the weakest common device on the market — light architecture, optimized code.
